About

Fewer tabs, better decisions

Oakridge Partners started from a simple frustration: comparing financial products means opening a dozen tabs, reading a dozen fee schedules, and still not being sure you picked well.

We built a short questionnaire that narrows the field to what's actually relevant to your situation — then gets out of the way. You still make the decision. We just save you the search.

How we choose what to show

Products are grouped into categories — credit, credit cards, insurance, mortgages, loans, banking, and business services. Your questionnaire answers map to one or more of those categories, and we show products from that category. We don't rank by which pays us more, and we don't hide options that pay us nothing.

How we make money

Some of the products we list pay us a referral commission if you sign up through our link. It doesn't change the price you pay. Full details are in our disclosure.
