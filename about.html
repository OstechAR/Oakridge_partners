About Oakridge Partners

Making financial information easier to understand through education, resources, and transparent partnerships.

Our Mission

Financial decisions can feel overwhelming. Many people struggle to understand credit, loans, banking products, and insurance options because information is often scattered or difficult to compare.


Oakridge Partners exists to organize financial education resources into a simpler experience, helping Canadians better understand topics that may affect their financial decisions.

How Oakridge Partners Works

1. Education First

We explain financial concepts in simple terms so users can build knowledge before exploring services.

2. Resource Discovery

Users can explore information related to their interests through educational pathways.

3. Partner Connections

When appropriate, users may be directed to third-party services that may be relevant to their interests.

What We Do Not Do

Oakridge Partners is not a bank, lender, insurance company, credit bureau, or financial advisory firm.


We do not:

  • Provide personalized financial advice.
  • Guarantee approval for financial products.
  • Make decisions on behalf of financial institutions.
  • Replace professional financial guidance.

Our Approach To Partnerships

We believe financial resources should be presented transparently. When users choose to explore certain products or services through partner links, Oakridge Partners may receive compensation.


Compensation helps support the operation of the platform and does not change our commitment to providing educational information.

Who We Help

People Comparing Options
