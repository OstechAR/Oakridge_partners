Understanding Credit in Canada

Credit plays an important role in many financial decisions. Learn how credit scores, credit reports, and credit history work so you can better understand your financial options.

What Is Credit?

Credit is the ability to borrow money or access financial services with the expectation that repayment will happen in the future.


Your credit history provides information about how you have managed borrowed money in the past. Companies called credit bureaus collect information and create credit reports that may be used by lenders and other organizations.

What Is A Credit Score?

A credit score is a number calculated using information from your credit history. Different organizations may use different scoring models, so your score can vary depending on where it is obtained.


Generally, credit scores are designed to help organizations evaluate how someone has managed credit obligations in the past.

Factors That Can Influence Credit

Payment History

Making payments on time is generally considered an important part of maintaining a positive credit history.

Credit Utilization

The amount of available credit being used can influence how credit information is evaluated.

Credit History Length

The length of time credit accounts have existed may be considered when evaluating credit history.

Credit Applications

Multiple credit applications in a short period may affect credit information.

Understanding Credit Reports

A credit report contains information about your credit accounts and payment history. Reviewing your credit report can help you understand what information organizations may see.


Canadians can request access to their credit information from the major credit bureaus operating in Canada.

Common Credit Questions

How long does it take to build credit?

Building credit history generally takes time and depends on individual circumstances and financial behaviour.


Does checking my credit affect my score?

Different types of credit checks can affect credit information differently. Understanding the difference between inquiries can help you make informed decisions.


Can credit be improved?

Many people work on improving their credit profile by learning how credit works and managing financial obligations responsibly.
