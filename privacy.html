What we store

Your questionnaire answers are saved in your browser's local storage so the results page can read them. They are not sent to, or stored on, an Oakridge Partners server. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are visited and which questionnaire steps people drop off at. This is aggregate, not tied to your name or contact details.

Third-party links

When you click through to a product provider, you leave this site and are subject to that provider's own privacy policy. We don't control, and aren't responsible for, how they handle your data.
